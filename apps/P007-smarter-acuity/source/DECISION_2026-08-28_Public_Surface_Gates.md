# DECISION 2026-08-28 — Public Surface Gates

Status: **operator-issued standing law** (this conversation, 28 Aug 2026).  
Project: P007 SMARTER Acuity.  
This file replaces the public-facing quality stack in Master Context v2.3 §4 and tightens how AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md / PROVENANCE.md apply to **posts, page ledes, shorts, and other shareable surfaces**.

It does **not** delete the weekday map, the monthly roots, open load-bearing, the claims ledger, or the operator GO for deploy and posting.

---

## 1. Why the old gates failed

Master Context v2.3 required four checks before publish: Accuracy · Ideological Fit · Platform Fit · Scholarly Integrity.

In practice the last check ate the other three. Agents treated “scholarly integrity” as:

- a source on every sentence
- primary-source antiquity as the default register
- no reach outside the stamped corpus
- every interpretive line naming and answering a counter-position (PROVENANCE R5)

The corpus and the Drive research folders were thin. The safe public-domain object was Vitruvius. Grok Bot filled the September pages with atmosphere (“a room is an argument,” “the face used here,” “a public bowl becomes part of the air”). The Screenwriter did not write that copy and does not want it.

The result was content that was careful and irrelevant. Nobody would follow an account to read it. Nobody would share it.

The weekday map is not the problem. The problem is what was allowed to count as a finished draft.

## 3. What is repealed for public surfaces

These rules no longer govern X captions, Instagram captions, YouTube titles/descriptions, page ledes, or short-video scripts:

1. **Scholarly Integrity as the first filter.** Accuracy remains. Citation spray does not.
2. **“Primary-source grounded” as the opening requirement.** A page may open on a live fight and carry the document below the fold.
3. **“Agents may not reach outside the monorepo for any other source material.”** Drafting may use free public URLs and the SMARTER Acuity Resources Notion page. Adding a source to `/corpus/` still needs operator GO.
4. **R5 on captions and ledes.** Naming and answering a counter-position is required on ebook chapters and on page sections that load-bear an interpretive thesis. It is not required on a 40-word post.
5. **Pair-map language in public copy.** “Aesthetics → Architecture,” “dual-post weave,” “Packet A,” “the face used here,” “claim row” are filing labels. They do not ship.
6. **Antiquity-only as a safe default.** Vitruvius, the Colosseum, and Rome remain legal objects. They are not sufficient. A week that never touches a living fight has failed the gate.

Master Context §4 gates are replaced by §4 below for every public surface. Ideological Fit is restated: pro–Western Civilization, liberty-rooted, anti-authoritarian, not snide toward persuadable readers — shown in the object, not chanted in the caption.

## 4. The new public-surface gate (all five)

A draft may not be offered as a post, lede, or short unless it clears **all five**.

### G1 · Relevant
A reader in 2026 has a reason to care today. That reason is a live fight or a fact they will use this week.

Live fights already in market (not exhaustive): what we build vs what people prefer; Western Civ dropped from the syllabus; 1619 / Zinn vs the documents; Ferguson’s six killer apps and the numbers behind them; published sport rules, records, eligibility; campus speech codes; civilizational pressure on Western institutions (Clash of Civilizations as news, not as a book report).

Fail: atmosphere about “rooms” and “bowls” with no building, no school, no rule, no number.

### G2 · Informative
One reusable piece of knowledge: a number, a named document, a working list, a mechanism.

Fail: a mood. Fail: a slogan.

### G3 · Helpful, inspiring, or a defense that is not on the nose
At least one of:

- **Helpful** — the reader can use it (how to judge a hall, how to read a textbook vs a page, what the 1863 Laws actually did).
- **Inspiring** — a made thing, a solved problem, a life with a turn. Not a pep rally.
- **Defense** — the inheritance is visible in the object. Do not write “defend Western Civilization” in the caption. Show the institution, the rule, the building, the record.

Fail: “Western Civilization is under assault” with no object. Fail: on-the-nose chanting.

### G4 · Shareable
Line 1 is the fact or the fight. Grade 8–10 English. One keepable object (list, cutaway, two photos, table, exam). Media that works muted. 40- or 80-word caption.

Fail: lecture-first. Fail: poetic fog. Fail: pair arrows.

### G5 · Source discipline without smothering
- Do not invent.
- Source numbers, dates, quotes, factual claims — or mark `TODO: SOURCE`.
- One good public source may carry a paragraph of meaning, context, application, or profundity.
- Captions cite nothing. The page carries the source below the fold.
- Contemporary scholars on the Resources page are in play (Ferguson, Hankins, Magness, Scruton, Guttmann, Sowell, Pieper, Huntington, McCloskey, and the rest of that roster). History is not the only register.
- Tagging a living academic is an operator decision. Never imply they work for this project.

Fail: unsourced statistic. Fail: a footnote on every sentence. Fail: refusing a contemporary fight because the name is not yet in `/corpus/`.

## 5. Dinner-table test (hard fail)

If a smart 17-year-old cannot retell the post in one sentence after hearing it once, it fails G1–G4 even if it is accurate and on-calendar.

Forbidden public diction (non-exhaustive): “a room is an argument,” “the face used here,” “claim row,” “dual-post weave,” “inhabitable,” “the record,” “postcard,” “mood you can hang on a wall.”

## 6. How this sits next to provenance

Provenance was built to stop circular citation. Keep that.

It was then used as a content diet. Stop that.

| Surface | Provenance intensity |
|---|---|
| X / IG caption, YT title | G1–G5 only. No claim-ID required on the caption. |
| Webpage | G1–G5 on the lede. Load-bearing facts on the page still need Tier 1/2 or `TODO: SOURCE`. R5 on the page’s interpretive thesis, not on every paragraph. |
| Ebook chapter | Full ledger. R5 on the chapter thesis. |

Agents may draft from free public URLs and from the Notion Resources page. They still may not invent. They still may not write to `/corpus/` without a GO.
