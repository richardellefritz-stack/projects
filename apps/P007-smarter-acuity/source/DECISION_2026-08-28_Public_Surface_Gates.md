### Amendment B · Published fights, quotation vs scene

Worked case: *Colin in Black & White* (Netflix, 2021). Inside Edition, 2 Nov 2021: “The former NFL player compares the league’s draft process to the slave auction block.” Head Case was right to bring that line to Screenwriter. The restriction that stalled the page was “the exact slogan must be a primary-source quotation already in `/corpus/`.” That restriction is repealed for public surfaces.

1. A public face is enough to sit the fight. Free, stable URL. Does not have to be in `/corpus/` first. A ten-second public news find is a legal door.
2. Sit three layers. Do not collapse them: spoken line / outlet caption or headline / image or staging. Putting “slave auction” in Kaepernick’s mouth as a quotation invents a sentence.
3. Verify with Screenwriter. Do not veto the topic.
4. Defense is the distinction, not the dunk. The West ended the Atlantic slave trade it had practiced. A published, voluntary, measured contest ending in a contract is not chattel. Do not write “own the libs.”
5. Still forbidden: invented quotes; treating a headline as a spoken sentence; posting; `/corpus/` writes without a GO. SmAq writes after the keepable card exists, not before.
