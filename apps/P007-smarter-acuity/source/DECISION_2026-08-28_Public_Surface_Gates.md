## 1. Why the old gates failed

Master Context v2.3 required four checks before publish: Accuracy · Ideological Fit · Platform Fit · Scholarly Integrity.

In practice the last check ate the other three. Agents treated “scholarly integrity” as:

- a source on every sentence
- primary-source antiquity as the default register
- no reach outside the stamped corpus
- every interpretive line naming and answering a counter-position (PROVENANCE R5)

The corpus and the Drive research folders were thin. The safe public-domain object was Vitruvius. Grok Bot filled the September pages with atmosphere. The Screenwriter did not write that copy and does not want it.

The result was content that was careful and irrelevant. Nobody would follow an account to read it. Nobody would share it.

The weekday map is not the problem. The problem is what was allowed to count as a finished draft.

## 3. What is repealed for public surfaces

These rules no longer govern X captions, Instagram captions, YouTube titles/descriptions, page ledes, or short-video scripts:

1. **Scholarly Integrity as the first filter.** Accuracy remains. Citation spray does not.
2. **“Primary-source grounded” as the opening requirement.** A page may open on a live fight and carry the document below the fold.
3. **“Agents may not reach outside the monorepo for any other source material.”** Drafting may use free public URLs and the SMARTER Acuity Resources Notion page. Adding a source to `/corpus/` still needs operator GO.
4. **R5 on captions and ledes.** Naming and answering a counter-position is required on ebook chapters and on page sections that load-bear an interpretive thesis. It is not required on a 40-word post.
5. **Pair-map language in public copy.** Filing labels do not ship.
6. **Atmosphere as a safe default.** Vitruvius, the Colosseum, and Rome remain legal objects. A week of usable older history can pass. A week of fog with no takeaway cannot.

## 4. The new public-surface gate (all five)

A draft may not be offered as a post, lede, or short unless it clears **all five**.

### G1 · Relevant
A living reader can see why this is worth a minute. “Living reader” is not “this week’s headline.” Relevance is perspective. Some readers think in news cycles. Some think in decades. Some think in centuries. The year on the calendar (2026, then 2027, then the next one) does not decide whether a fact is in bounds.

Any of these doors counts:

- a current fight
- something from the last five, ten, twenty, or fifty years that still structures the world they walk through
- an older object, rule, building, text, or life that a non-specialist can still use or recognize
- a peculiar fact they can repeat later

Timely news is one path. It is not the gate. Thousands of years of Western history can clear G1 if they also clear G2–G4. Vitruvius, 1863, 1665, and 1215 are legal. They fail only when they are atmosphere: no takeaway, no object, no sentence a person would bother to keep.

Fail: seminar fog with nothing a reader can reuse. Fail is not “too old.”

### G2 · Informative
One reusable piece of knowledge: a number, a named document, a working list, a mechanism.

Fail: a mood. Fail: a slogan.

### G3 · Helpful, inspiring, or a defense that is not on the nose
At least one of: helpful (the reader can use it); inspiring (a made thing, a solved problem, a life with a turn); defense shown in the object, not chanted in the caption.

### G4 · Shareable
Line 1 is the fact or the question. Grade 8–10 English. One keepable object. Media that works muted. 40- or 80-word caption.

### G5 · Source discipline without smothering
Do not invent. Source numbers, dates, quotes, factual claims — or mark `TODO: SOURCE`. One good public source may carry a paragraph of meaning. Captions cite nothing. Contemporary scholars on the Resources page are in play. History is not the only register. Tagging a living academic is an operator decision.

---

## 5. Dinner-table test (hard fail)

If a smart 17-year-old cannot retell the post in one sentence after hearing it once, it fails G1–G4 even if it is accurate and on-calendar. That sentence may be about 1863 or about last Tuesday.

---

## 6. How this sits next to provenance

Provenance was built to stop circular citation. Keep that. It was then used as a content diet. Stop that.

| Surface | Provenance intensity |
|---|---|
| X / IG caption, YT title | G1–G5 only. No claim-ID required on the caption. |
| Webpage | G1–G5 on the lede. Load-bearing facts still need Tier 1/2 or `TODO: SOURCE`. R5 on the page thesis, not on every paragraph. |
| Ebook chapter | Full ledger. R5 on the chapter thesis. |

Agents may draft from free public URLs and from the Notion Resources page. They still may not invent. They still may not write to `/corpus/` without a GO.
