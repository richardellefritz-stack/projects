# DECISION 2026-08-28 — Amendment B

Published fights, quotation vs scene. Same day as Amendment A.
Parent: `source/DECISION_2026-08-28_Public_Surface_Gates.md`.

Worked case: *Colin in Black & White* (Netflix, 2021). Inside Edition, 2 Nov 2021: “The former NFL player compares the league’s draft process to the slave auction block.” Head Case was right to bring that line to Screenwriter. The restriction that stalled the page was “the exact slogan must be a primary-source quotation already in `/corpus/`.” That restriction is repealed for public surfaces.

1. **A public face is enough to sit the fight.** A free, stable URL — outlet caption, on-camera line, documentary scene as the outlet describes it, official transcript — may open a page. It does not have to be in `/corpus/` first. Adding it to `/corpus/` is later, under operator GO. A ten-second public news find is a legal door.

2. **Sit three layers. Do not collapse them.**
   - what the person said on camera
   - what the outlet wrote as caption or headline
   - what the image or staging shows
   Inside Edition’s caption is Inside Edition’s sentence. Kaepernick’s on-camera line in that episode is the poke / prod / power-dynamic line. The auction is the scene the series cuts to. A page that puts “slave auction” in his mouth as a quotation has invented a sentence.

3. **Verify with Screenwriter. Do not veto the topic.** Head Case or Head Master bringing a contested living-person attribution to Screenwriter is correct. That is source hygiene. It is not a reason to drop a living fight and return to antiquity.

4. **Defense is the distinction, not the dunk.** This project answers narratives that erase what the West actually did — including that the West ended the Atlantic slave trade it had practiced, and that a published, voluntary, measured contest ending in a contract is not chattel. Show that distinction in the object. Do not write “own the libs.” A polemic with no keepable fact fails G2 and G3.

5. Still forbidden: invented quotes; treating a headline as a spoken sentence; posting; writing to `/corpus/` without a GO.
