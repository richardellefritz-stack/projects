# Rest the Amphitheatre Still Permits

A public bowl is for the hours when nobody is being asked to watch.

English *recreation* comes from Latin *recreare* / *recreatio*: to restore, to refresh, recovery. That is the face used here. It is a word-history, not a claim about what Roman amphitheatres did on empty days.

[CLM-RX-0001]

A civilization which cannot rest inside its own public rooms will treat every Saturday as a rehearsal for a crowd. People walk the rim and sit without being an audience. I have dismissed classes into a quad and watched them become a public without a program. That notice is not on the dictionary page.

## Leisure That Is Not Yet a Crowd

Evening leisure is easier to fake.

We will not tease a title to harvest a click. Leisure that is not yet a crowd is a Saturday that keeps its own hours.

The bowl is a member of the week, and the week is allowed to breathe. Go home.
