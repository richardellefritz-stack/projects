# The Amphitheatre as the Air

A public bowl becomes part of the air. People walk past it on ordinary days, hear it when they are not attending, and use it as a landmark, a shadow, a place the wind comes around. That is environment: the building as climate.

The empty days are most of its life. A city that keeps a bowl is choosing a kind of weather. Entertainment left alone pretends the building vanishes at dusk.

I have lived near stadiums and near churches. Both change the block when they are shut.

## The Amphitheatre as a Show

Then the lights, or the sun, and it is a show. People came to be gathered on purpose. That is entertainment in the old sense: to hold a city's attention for a measured time, and to send it home. We do not have an accepted claim row on this Friday, and we will not borrow one.

A show is a claim about what a people will sit still for. It can be play, or judgment, or both. The building does not decide the ethics by existing. Someone filled it, for an hour, with a reason to look.
