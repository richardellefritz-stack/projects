# 001 — First Product Opportunity

## Proposed Product

**"The Freelancer's Rate Reality Check"** — a research-backed niche ebook + companion interactive rate calculator, targeted at new/early-career freelancers (writers, designers, developers, VAs) who are underpricing their work.

- **Ebook**: A short, sharply-written guide (40–60 pages) built on aggregated, cited data on freelance rate benchmarks by skill/region/experience, the true cost of underpricing (taxes, downtime, scope creep, benefits gap), and a practical framework for setting and raising rates.
- **Companion tool**: A lightweight interactive web calculator where the reader inputs their desired income, working days, overhead, and niche, and gets a recommended hourly/project rate — with the ebook's framework embedded in the tool's explanations. Delivered as a simple web app (could live under `apps/` once greenlit).

## Why This One

- **High margin, low marginal cost**: Digital-only, no inventory, near-zero reproduction cost once built.
- **Evergreen, recurring pain point**: New freelancers enter the market constantly; underpricing is a near-universal early mistake, so the audience refreshes itself.
- **Proven format**: Ebook + calculator/tool pairings convert well because the free-feeling interactive tool is the lead magnet/upsell hook, and the ebook is the monetizable depth product (or vice versa — tool gated behind ebook purchase).
- **Showcases the full pipeline well**: Requires real research (rate data, sourcing), a clear architecture (content + tool spec), genuinely parallelizable generation (content chapters vs. tool build are independent workstreams), and an objectively checkable quality gate (are the numbers right, does the calculator work, is the writing tight).
- **Fast to produce**: No licensing, interviews, or long lead-time research required — rate benchmark data is publicly aggregable, and the tool is a straightforward calculator, not a complex app.
- **Natural upsell path**: Rate calculator → ebook → future products (contract templates, invoicing guide, etc.) for the same audience, setting up Feedback → next Opportunity Sensing cycle.

## Format Category

Research-backed niche ebook + companion interactive tool (hybrid), as preferred for this pipeline's first run.
